# Business Performance Dashboard

## Project overview
A business analytics dashboard designed to track key business performance indicators including revenue, costs, profit, customer acquisition and customer churn.

## Deliverables
- `business_performance_data.csv` — sample business dataset
- `Business_Performance_Dashboard.xlsx` — Excel dashboard with KPI summary, monthly analysis and charts
- `index.html` — browser-based dashboard demo

## Key KPIs
- Total Revenue: $857,241.50
- Total Cost: $498,675.42
- Total Profit: $358,566.08
- New Customers: 3,051
- Churned Customers: 442

## What I built
The project organizes monthly business data by region and product, calculates revenue, cost and profit, and presents the results through KPI cards, trend charts and regional comparisons.

## What I learned
I learned how business metrics can be organized into a dashboard, how to compare revenue and costs, how to monitor profit, and how customer acquisition and churn can be used to understand business performance.

## Tools
Excel / CSV and a standalone HTML dashboard demo.

## Note
The dataset is synthetic and created for educational/project demonstration purposes.
